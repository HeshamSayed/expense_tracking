# Privacy Policy for MoneyGuard

**Last Updated: November 4, 2025**

## Introduction

MoneyGuard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: Email address, name, password (encrypted)
- **Financial Data**: Transaction records, account balances, budget information, categories
- **Profile Information**: Timezone, locale, currency preferences
- **Payment Information**: Processed securely through Stripe (we never store credit card details)

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: App features used, frequency of use, session duration
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, app crashes, performance data

### Information from Third Parties

- **Payment Processors**: Stripe provides subscription status and payment confirmations
- **Ad Networks**: Google AdMob (for free tier users who consent to personalized ads)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain our expense tracking services
- Process transactions and manage your account
- Send transaction summaries and budget alerts (if enabled)
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Provide customer support
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures

- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Authentication**: Secure JWT token-based authentication
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access, principle of least privilege
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and vulnerability scanning
- **Backup**: Regular encrypted backups with 30-day retention

### Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: Data permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Transaction History**: Retained for 7 years for financial record purposes (unless you request deletion)

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT:

- ❌ Sell your personal data to third parties
- ❌ Share your financial data with advertisers
- ❌ Use your transaction data for marketing purposes
- ❌ Provide your information to data brokers

### We MAY Share Data With:

- **Service Providers**: Stripe (payments), SendGrid (emails), AWS (hosting) - under strict data protection agreements
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or government regulations
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale (you will be notified)
- **With Your Consent**: Any other sharing requires your explicit consent

## Your Privacy Rights

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)

- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection**: Object to data processing
- **Restriction**: Restrict how we process your data

### CCPA Rights (California Users)

- **Know**: What personal information we collect and how it's used
- **Delete**: Request deletion of personal information
- **Opt-Out**: Opt-out of data sale (we don't sell data)
- **Non-Discrimination**: We won't discriminate for exercising your rights

### Exercising Your Rights

Email: privacy@moneyguard.com
Response Time: Within 30 days
Identity Verification: Required for security

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MoneyGuard is NOT directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at privacy@moneyguard.com.

## Advertising and Analytics

### Free Tier Users

- **Google AdMob**: Displays ads in the free version
- **Ad Personalization**: Optional (you can opt-out in settings)
- **Ad Data**: AdMob may collect device identifiers, ad interaction data
- **Control**: Disable personalized ads in Settings → Privacy

### Pro Subscribers

- **No Ads**: Pro subscribers see no advertisements
- **Analytics Only**: Anonymized usage analytics for app improvement

## Cookies and Tracking

We use:

- **Essential Cookies**: For authentication and security
- **Analytics Cookies**: To understand app usage (anonymized)
- **No Tracking**: We do not use cross-site tracking technologies

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure adequate safeguards through:

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Privacy Shield Framework (where applicable)
- Adequate data protection agreements

## Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach:

- We will notify affected users within 72 hours
- Notification will include nature of breach and steps to protect yourself
- We will report to relevant authorities as required by law

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will:

- Notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification
- Post the updated policy with a new "Last Updated" date
- Obtain consent for material changes (where required by law)
